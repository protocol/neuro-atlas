# Science, Paradromics and Precision — company-blog review

Reviewed October 15, 2026. Starting points were the official company sites linked from [PL Neuro's Ecosystem Allies](https://www.plneuro.xyz/ecosystem-allies/): [Science news](https://science.xyz/news/), [Paradromics news](https://paradromics.com/news/) and [blog](https://paradromics.com/blog/), and [Precision news and research](https://www.precisionneuro.io/articles). Precision's current news cards link to its company-authored GlobeNewswire releases.

This is a bounded 2026 milestone review of those three existing companies, not a full historical backfill or a new coverage date for the entire Atlas. Announcement, publication and procedure dates are kept distinct. The additions appear in the **Milestone timeline**; no funding, patient-count, recording-hours or field-velocity series is changed.

## Added: Science Corporation

- **July 22 — two PRIMA Humanitarian Use Device designations.** The [company announcement](https://science.xyz/news/hud-designation/) states: “PRIMA received two Humanitarian Use Device (HUD) designations,” covering advanced geographic atrophy and Stargardt disease. July 22 is the announcement date; the [October 13 update](https://science.xyz/news/stargardt-first-rehab/) says the grants were in spring but supplies no exact dates. [BrightFocus](https://www.brightfocus.org/resource/prima-implant-brings-vision-restoration-within-reach/) corroborates the designations, but its July grant wording is not used to override Science's later timing. [FDA's program description](https://www.fda.gov/industry/medical-products-rare-diseases-and-conditions/humanitarian-use-device-hud-designation-program) distinguishes designation from a subsequent HDE marketing application. Activity `HUD`, clinical pathway, not marketing approval; glossary expansion added.
- **October 5 — PRIMAvera 36-month follow-up announcement.** The [full company report](https://science.xyz/news/long-term-prima-results/) says: “At 36 months, 27 patients were evaluable for safety and 18 for efficacy,” from 38 originally implanted; it reports mean improvement of 29 ETDRS letters and 14 of 18 with at least 15-letter improvement. These denominators remain attached. The [EURETINA program](https://euretina.org/vienna-2026/satellite-symposia/prima-retinal-implant-in-geographic-atrophy-pivotal-trial-evidence-and-36-month-outcomes/) corroborates presentation of 36-month outcomes, not the numerical results independently. October 5 is the release date, not an inferred conference-session date; this is company-reported conference follow-up, not a newly peer-reviewed trial report or FDA approval.
- **October 13 — first Stargardt participant's early rehabilitation disclosure.** The [company update](https://science.xyz/news/stargardt-first-rehab/) says: “Our first patient with Stargardt disease received the PRIMA implant in May” and describes a letter seen in session one and a word read in session two. The timeline uses the disclosure date and explicitly retains **May 2026** implantation timing. [PRIMAlia NCT07266584](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07266584) corroborates the exploratory study and Sydney Eye Hospital site, not the individual outcome; its [official record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07266584) has no posted results. This is an attributed single-patient observation, not trial-wide efficacy or Stargardt commercial authorization.

## Added: Paradromics

- **June 17 — first chronic Connect-One implant announced.** The [company release](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-completes-first-human-brain-computer-interface-bci-implantation/) announces “completion of the first surgical implantation” in Connect-One at University of Michigan Health. [Michigan Medicine's institutional release](https://www.newswise.com/articles/university-of-michigan-implants-first-in-human-paradromics-wireless-brain-computer-interface-designed-to-restore-communication) independently confirms the first participant and distinguishes the **temporary 2025 intraoperative implant**. [NCT07357428](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07357428) corroborates the study design. June 17 is the announcement, not a separately verified surgery day. A separate `trial` marker replaces reliance on a June implant claim buried in the March APEX note.
- **October 8 — four-year preclinical durability milestone.** The [announcement](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-demonstrates-four-years-of-stable-neural-recording-in-major-brain-computer-interface-durability-milestone/) explicitly identifies “two sheep implanted with Paradromics cortical modules representative of the electrode technology.” The [technical blog](https://paradromics.com/blog/preclinical-research/) retains its original 2025 publication date but says it was updated October 8, 2026; example traces are at 1,470 and 1,443 days. It also shows a recording pause: this is stable **longitudinal**, not uninterrupted, recording. A separate six-sheep full-platform cohort is nearly two years. No independent four-year peer-reviewed paper was established. Activity is explicitly `preclinical` within the existing clinical/development lane, not a new stage or a claim of human durability.

## Added: Precision Neuroscience

- **February 1 — acute four-patient feasibility paper.** The official Research page links the [Neurosurgical Focus study, available as an institutional abstract](https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/initial-experience-with-the-precision-neuroscience-layer-7-micro-/). It describes four awake-craniotomy patients, four-word speech classification of 77.5% in one participant, and four-direction motor classification of 78–84%. [Europe PMC metadata](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:41621103&format=json) gives February 1 as first publication date, not procedure timing. The [July erratum](https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/erratum-initial-experience-with-the-precision-neuroscience-layer-) adds contributor acknowledgments, not an outcome correction. No unrestricted speech restoration or chronic efficacy is inferred. Publisher full text was blocked; the institutional abstract and indexed metadata were retrieved.
- **April 30 — UChicago Medicine clinical research partnership.** The [company release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/30/3284938/0/en/precision-neuroscience-partners-with-uchicago-medicine-researchers-to-advance-brain-computer-interface-research.html), quoting UChicago's investigator, says the parties “have launched a clinical partnership” in AI-driven sensorimotor function and neuroprosthetics. It is research-network expansion, not completed efficacy, financing or new clearance. April 30 is the announcement date.
- **October 8 — human CNS atlas manuscript announcement.** The [release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/08/3377330/0/en/precision-neuroscience-unveils-a-new-map-of-the-human-nervous-system.html) reports 70 patients at 12 academic centers and “more than 180 hours of synchronized neural and behavioral data.” Separately, its wider program had surpassed 100 implanted patients. The [official bioRxiv API](https://api.biorxiv.org/details/biorxiv/10.64898/2026.09.24.754261) supplies the abstract and **October 1** v1 posting date; October 8 is the announcement, not preprint publication. The full preprint was blocked, so detailed hours/site counts remain attributed to the company release. Do not infer an openly downloadable dataset, 100 permanent implants, simultaneous whole-brain recording, or completed whole-brain coverage. The September 24 financing release already reported over 100 **procedures**; no second standalone threshold marker is added.

## Corrected existing entries

The distinct June event now has its own marker, and the note no longer treats June 17 as the surgery date.
- **Paradromics device-pathway expansion, August 26:** the [approval announcement](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-receives-fda-approval-to-expand-bci-access-to-personal-computing-devices-in-connect-one-clinical-study/) permits adding compatible personal devices **after the agreed deployment/testing process**. The note now distinguishes this from marketing approval or completed use on every device type.
- **Precision–Medtronic, January 12:** `JV` → `partner`. The [release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/12/3217312/0/en/precision-neuroscience-and-medtronic-partner-to-integrate-layer-7-brain-computer-interface-with-medtronic-neurosurgical-platform.html) says “will co-develop” a Layer 7 / StealthStation solution; no joint venture or completed deployment is established.
- **Precision clearance, April 17, 2025 announcement:** the [FDA K242618 letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf24/K242618.pdf) is dated **March 30, 2025**, identifies Class II **Layer 7-T**, and specifies temporary use **less than 30 days**. It calls this a passive device without wireless capability or software. The existing announcement date is retained and labeled; the note no longer suggests clearance of the complete chronic wireless BCI.
