### Synchron — voluntary enrollment pause, reported November 12

[WIRED original reporting](https://www.wired.com/story/synchron-pauses-trials-of-its-brain-implant-due-to-performance-issues/) reproduces Synchron’s statement that it voluntarily paused enrollment after identifying “a performance issue involving the lead.” Synchron said no device-related safety issues had been identified and that the pause was “not an FDA-directed action”; these are attributed company claims, not regulator findings. Whole-system hardware upgrades were planned before enrollment resumes; no restart date was announced.

**Reporting source / reported date:** November 12 is WIRED’s publication date, not the undisclosed pause-decision day. Sponsor-submitted [INTENT / NCT07543367](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07543367) and [FOCUS-AUS / NCT07533903](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07533903) registry records corroborate active/not-recruiting status, not the precise cause or decision date. [FOCUS-CAN / NCT07446114](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07446114) separately says terminated for **insufficient recruitment**; it is not attributed to the lead issue. No FDA hold, pivotal-trial start, approval revocation, or new regulatory marker is asserted.
