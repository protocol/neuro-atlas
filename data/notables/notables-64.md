# Neurotech Notables #64 — core-company review

Source issue: *Neurotech Notables #64: Oct 1–15, 2026*, published October 15, 2026.[1]

## Added to the Milestone timeline

- **Newronika — pivotal clinical study:** Newronika announced that the first ADVENT participant received an αDBS implant in Monza on September 21, 2026; the company announced the procedure September 30.[2]
  ClinicalTrials.gov identifies ADVENT as a recruiting, randomized, double-blind crossover device study and records an actual August 26, 2026 start date.[3]
  This is a first-procedure milestone, not a U.S. clearance, approval, or efficacy result.[2][3]
- **Neuralink — decoder research:** Neuralink reported that participants had streamed more than 50,000 hours of unlabeled neural recordings by October 1.[4]
  Its participant-specific neural encoders were pretrained on thousands of hours of each participant's own recordings; separate reporting records six personal cursor-test bests, including 11.32 BPS for one participant.[4][5]
  This is recorded as research progress, not peer-reviewed evidence, a regulatory event, or funding.[4][5]
- **INBRAIN Neuroelectronics — MINIGRAPH preclinical milestone:** The MINIGRAPH consortium announced project completion on October 7, including an integrated prototype and a magnetic robotic implantation procedure validated in vitro and in a large-animal translational model.[6][7]
  The European Commission project record confirms INBRAIN's participation and the project’s scope of closed-loop graphene implants and minimally invasive implantation.[8]
  This is preclinical; it does not represent a human implantation, clearance, or commercial launch.[7][8]

## Reviewed but not added

- **Science Corporation — PRIMA 36-month results:** the results are material clinical progress, but the same 36-month PRIMA event is already represented in the canonical timeline; this review adds no duplicate.[9]
- **Paradromics — four-year recording durability:** the company reports more than four years of stable recording and decoding in two implanted sheep, but the same preclinical durability event is already represented; this review adds no duplicate.[10]
- **Precision Neuroscience — nervous-system atlas:** the 70-patient atlas announcement is already represented in the canonical timeline; the related September 24 $250 million Series D was also already present and was not re-counted.[11]
- **Science Corp. — September 17 president appointment:** the existing legacy leadership row is not modified or re-counted; routine leadership appointments are not a Notables #64 timeline addition.[1]
- **Attune's VANTUS study, NeurAxis coverage, and other non-roster items:** omitted because the live Funding index company roster is the governing scope and no companies were added.[1]
