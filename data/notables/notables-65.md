Source: [Notables #65: Oct 16–31, 2026](https://neurotechnology.substack.com/p/notables-65), published November 2, 2026. Reviewed November 9 against the current 25-company Funding index roster, canonical data and all open PR diffs. No roster expansion.

## Milestone timeline additions

- **Coherence Neuro — October 21, $45 million Series A.** The [company announcement](https://coherenceneuro.substack.com/p/inventing-cancer-therapy-that-learns) says “Announcing our $45m Series A co-led by Founders Fund and The OpenAI Startup Fund.” The [company-site version](https://www.coherenceneuro.com/article/inventing-cancer-therapy-that-learns) confirms “backed Coherence with $45m.” This is new round capital, not valuation or cumulative funding. Its chronic human trial and FDA Breakthrough Device designation are future goals, not accomplished regulatory events.
- **NeuroXess — October 28, RMB 700 million Series B.** The [company announcement](https://www.neuroxess.com/news/naohukejiwancheng7yiyuanblunrongziguotoujulishenchuangtouyuqinzhizibenlingtou/) states “今日宣布完成7亿元人民币B轮融资” (“today announced completion of RMB 700 million Series B financing”). The native amount is retained and converted to an approximately **$105 million historical estimate** using the [Federal Reserve H.10 observation for October 28](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/20261102/): **CNY 6.6662 per USD**, or `700 / 6.6662 = 105.00735051453601` USD million. This is explicitly an **announcement-date fallback**: the actual investment/cash-closing date is undisclosed. It is not a company-reported USD figure or today’s rate. The estimate is included once in each view’s own capital total, not added across the two views. Machine-readable native amount, rate, date basis and source live in `data/funding-index/fx-conversions.json`; generation derives both views from that one pinned observation.
- **Paradromics — October 19, Siemens Healthineers partnership.** Its [announcement](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-siemens-healthineers-launch-strategic-initiative-build-advanced-imaging-foundation-for-the-future-of-brain-computer-interfaces/) says the companies “will develop an integrated imaging approach spanning the continuum of BCI care.” Material development collaboration, not an already deployed or cleared integrated product.
- **Paradromics — October 26, expanded Connect-One recruitment.** The [company announcement](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-receives-fda-approval-to-expand-connexus-brain-computer-interface-clinical-study-recruitment-nationwide/) describes an FDA-approved protocol amendment “removing the requirement that prospective participants live within a four-hour drive.” Other eligibility and travel requirements remain. This expands an existing IDE study; it is neither initial IDE authorization nor marketing approval. No additional Funding index regulatory marker is created for this amendment.
- **Precision Neuroscience — October 21, first international clinical research site.** The [company-issued release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/29/3391322/0/en/precision-neuroscience-expands-clinical-network-beyond-the-u-s-with-first-international-site-in-abu-dhabi.html) identifies a two-year Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi MOU: “The agreement, signed October 21st,” makes it the first research site outside the U.S. The event uses the signing date; the announcement was October 29. Not a new clearance, commercial launch or completed efficacy result.
- **Neuralink — October 28, preliminary VOICE speech results.** Its [dated company post](https://x.com/neuralink/status/2115534593147187397) reports “One participant achieved 75 WPM at 5.8% WER on a 125k-word vocabulary in their first week.” A [company follow-up](https://x.com/neuralink/status/2115534595009499154) identifies the October 28 NEALS presentation. Recorded as preliminary, company-reported single-participant research, not independent validation or regulatory approval. The linked conference abstract could not be read through the available extraction route; no unobserved abstract detail is asserted.
