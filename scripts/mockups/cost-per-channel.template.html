FOR DISCUSSION

Should Atlas track the cost layers separately?

A cheaper headstage is not the same as a cheaper recording setup. This view keeps probe prices, readout electronics, and matched complete setups apart—and leaves the last one open until the evidence is there.

CONTEXT · NOT PLOTTED

Useful references, different denominators

Transport capacity

Original SciFi · 2024

$1,024 ÷ ~5,200 bandwidth-equivalent channels ≈ $0.20 prices headstage transport capacity, not a populated recording system. It is not the current SciFi 2 price.

Signal mode matters

NYX / Axon

512 electrode inputs and 256 differential amplifiers are different counts. Optical-stimulation LEDs are not recording channels.

Historical context

Open-source hardware · 2015

Siegle et al., Figure 1, report a nearly 100-fold decrease over 20 years for selected open-source platforms. Not digitized here; not a Blackrock-to-Science factor.

Unmatched configuration

Modern NHP recording · 2025

A reported $7,000–15,000 system-cost range has no matched priced channel configuration in this snapshot. No ratio is plotted.

Measurement contract & limits

Denominator: simultaneously acquired, spike-capable electrical recording channels in the priced configuration. Selectable sites, connector pins, AP/LFP bands, optical LEDs, isolated neurons, and transport capacity are not interchangeable counts. No equivalence to ECoG direct-spike readout is assumed.

Accounting: hardware purchase prices or itemized builds, not manufacturing cost or clinical-procedure cost. USD and EUR remain separate; no inflation adjustment, exchange-rate conversion, lifetime assumptions, or session amortization. The samples are selected public benchmarks, not a market census.

Dates: historical points use paper or report publication year; current prices use the November 5, 2026 observation date. Each evidence record retains its actual price-date basis. All data are a fixed discussion snapshot, not live quotes or an availability promise.

Complete setup: no matched, same-configuration price points are established. Missing is not zero. In particular, the NP 2.0 probe price cannot be combined with an NP 1.0 adapter into an exact full-setup price.
