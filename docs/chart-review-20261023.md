- **Acquisition economics:** display `1 / published voxels-per-dollar` as dollars per voxel. The nine source values remain in `docs/data/acquisition-economics-table-a1.json`; no re-estimation from throughput/hourly cost. Optical is purple, EM orange; prospective PEEM remains labeled. These are rough acquisition estimates, not all-in mapping cost. [Source Table A1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.25713v1), printed page 18, independently reread. - **Reconstruction error:** one 12-observation human/mouse split–merge plot, preserving all workbook decimals. Species color and dataset shape distinguish H01 multiSEM, mouse Si150L4 multiSEM, and mouse SBEM. All are from the [RoboEM 2024 source-data workbook](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41592-024-02226-5/MediaObjects/41592_2024_2226_MOESM6_ESM.xlsx). These are operating points, not independent species replicates; no regression line is fitted. The prior mouse fragment aliases the combined plot without duplicate marks.
- **Chronic intracortical recipients:** retain all Neuralink/Paradromics source checkpoints and add the [14-person BrainGate safety cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10074470/) published in 2023, observed 2004–2021. No current-program-total inference, sums or active-user claim. The Paradromics legacy fragment aliases the combined plot.
- **Temporary surface recordings:** [Precision 37 patients, April 17, 2025](https://www.precisionneuro.io/articles/company-news/precision-neuroscience-receives-fda-clearance-for-high-resolution-cortical-electrode-array), and [>50, October 2, 2025](https://www.precisionneuro.io/articles/company-news/precision-neuroscience-reports-first-high-bandwidth-brain-computer-interface-achieved-without-open-surgery), separate from chronic recipients. The [October 2026 >100 claim](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/08/3377330/0/en/precision-neuroscience-unveils-a-new-map-of-the-human-nervous-system.html) has mixed CNS scope and lacks a brain-only split. [Motif’s two-person study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11014439/) is acute stimulation, not chronic recording-BCI adoption; retain as context, not a plotted recording count.
- **Company-held intracortical hours:** add [Neuralink’s October 1, 2026 >50,000 accumulated streamed hours](https://neuralink.com/updates/pretraining-on-50000-hours/) separately from public/controlled-access EEG and other modalities. This is not the amount used to train one pooled model; the post says live-use models were participant-specific. Preserve `>` in visible values. - **Structure/function:** visible method/source context for Lee 2016 (50 identities, including four apical dendrites), Turner 2022 (112 cells), and MICrONS 2025 (15,439 unique neurons). The overlapping 37,364 automated matches at 83% precision remain separate context, never another independent time-series observation.
- **Public data:** add [FAFB’s published 12 TB downloadable image representation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC6063995/fullTextXML), 2018, and [Hildebrand’s ~2.7 TB compressed aligned larval-zebrafish imagery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5594570/), 2017. Data-product sizes are not homogeneous or additive.
