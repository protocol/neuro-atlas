# Third-party material and licensing scope

The project's **Apache-2.0 OR MIT** choice covers original software and original documentation to the extent the contributors hold those rights. It is **not a blanket license** for everything in this repository or everything linked by the website. Retain each existing copyright notice, attribution, and upstream license. If a file or source has separate terms, those terms continue to govern that material.

## Research data and source material

Scientific measurements, research data, dataset extracts, source compilations, article text, papers, figures, screenshots of third-party work, and imported fixtures retain the rights and terms of their respective sources. Citation or public availability is not itself permission to redistribute. The software license does not grant access to controlled or private research datasets and does not make an upstream collection open-licensed.

Examples of mixed-source areas include `data/`, `src/data/`, `docs/bci-study-data/`, source documents, and chart evidence fixtures. Some records are original annotations or derivations; others reproduce source material. **Do not infer a single data license from a directory name.** Consult point-level source URLs, provenance documents, dataset/version metadata, and existing notices. Facts and calculations may have a different legal status from the collection or presentation; no new upstream rights are asserted here.

## Existing separately licensed source

`docs/sources/recording-scaling/LICENSE` supplies **CC-BY-4.0** terms for that retained source snapshot, including its copied `scaling.py` and data. Preserve that local license, source attribution, and modification information; do not relabel the copied Python file Apache/MIT just because it is code. This does not establish that every underlying paper or cited measurement uses CC-BY-4.0. The source and transformation context is documented in [docs/performance-curves.md](docs/performance-curves.md).

Logo provenance is recorded in `data/logos/manifest.csv` and `docs/source-logo-provenance.json`, with source-brand assets also under `public/source-logos/`. Those records establish origin/retrieval, not blanket reuse permission or trademark clearance.

## Logos, artwork, fonts, and trademarks

Company and institutional logos in `data/logos/`, `public/logos/`, source-brand assets, third-party artwork, and fonts are not relicensed by the project's code license. Retain their source/provenance and any bundled license. Fonts with their own licenses remain under those licenses. Protocol Labs, PL Neuro, company names, logos, and other trademarks are not licensed for endorsement or branding by this grant.

## Dependencies and adapted code

Dependencies retain their own licenses, discoverable in the installed packages and corresponding upstream repositories. Any separately identified copied/adapted code retains its original terms and notices; this dual-license choice does not override them. Keep attribution with any redistributable adapted material.

## Before adding or reusing material

Record the primary source, exact release/version, access date, applicable license or restrictions, and attribution in the relevant provenance record. Only upload material you have the right to submit. When redistribution rights are unknown, link to the original and flag the uncertainty for review instead of uploading a copy or treating it as Apache/MIT.

This is a scope and preservation guide, **not a completed rights audit or clearance inventory**. Existing source-level notices remain authoritative. Report a missing attribution or uncertain permission through a sourced issue or the site's Feedback control; report private/security material through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
